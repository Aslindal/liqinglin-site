关于我About

在规则的两端On Both Sides of the Rule

我是李青林，在四川出生长大。本科在成都理工大学学经济学；之后在北京大学国际法学院读了四年，同时攻读美国法 Juris Doctor（全英文授课）与中国法法律硕士——一套普通法训练，一套中国法训练。

I am Qinglin Li, born and raised in Sichuan. I studied economics at Chengdu University of Technology, then spent four years at Peking University School of Transnational Law, completing a U.S.-law Juris Doctor (taught entirely in English) and a Juris Master in Chinese law — one in the common law, one in Chinese law.

我的职业走过一个来回：先在企业调查舞弊，后到律所参与刑事辩护，再回到企业。查明者与辩护者互为镜像，我在规则的两端各坐过一次。两端的经历教会我一件事：规则不偏袒任何一方，它只偏袒证据。如今我专注于企业反舞弊调查与合规体系建设——风险识别、证据链构建、数据取证、访谈与报告——独立完成从线索到结论的整个调查闭环，并推动多起案件进入刑事程序。合规不只是惩戒，更是借制度与文化，让组织少犯错误。偶然不可根除，只能一次次被驯服。

My career has been a round trip: first investigating fraud inside companies, then joining a law firm in criminal defense, then returning to the corporate side. Investigator and defender are mirror images of each other — I have sat once on each side of the rule. Both sides taught me one thing: the rule favors no one; it favors only evidence. Today I focus on corporate fraud investigation and compliance systems — risk detection, evidence chains, digital forensics, interviews, and reports — carrying each case from first clue to final conclusion, and pushing multiple cases into criminal proceedings. Compliance is not merely punishment; it is systems and culture that help organizations make fewer mistakes. Chance can never be rooted out — only tamed, again and again.

工作之外Beyond the Work

怀疑是工作，热爱是生活Doubt Is My Work, Love Is My Life

怀疑做久了会成习惯，所以我在证据之外留了一间不设防的房间：播客、篮球、骑行，以及哲学与法律理论带来的漫长思考，都在里面。走进这间房，不必出示任何证据。它提醒我：规则与证据之外，还有人的温度。

Doubt hardens into habit, so beyond the evidence I keep a room with the door unlocked: podcasts, basketball, cycling, and the long thoughts that philosophy and legal theory invite. Whoever steps in need not produce a single exhibit. It reminds me that beyond rules and evidence there is human warmth.
